Privacy Policy

Prop Consistency Tracker · Last updated: July 12, 2026

Prop Consistency Tracker ("the app", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

What information we collect

  • Google account (optional): if you sign in with Google, we collect your email address to identify your account.
  • Data from your evaluation accounts/challenges: the parameters you configure (account size, targets, drawdown, etc.) and the trades/results you log manually. This is practice data you enter yourself — the app does not connect to any broker or real financial account.
  • Subscription information: if you purchase the Premium version, we process your subscription status through Google Play and RevenueCat (our subscription management provider). We do not store payment card data.
  • Advertising identifiers: if you see ads within the app (free version), Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data under its own privacy policy.

What we use your information for

  • Syncing your accounts and trades across devices when you sign in.
  • Validating and managing your Premium subscription.
  • Showing ads in the free version (where applicable).
  • Providing support when you contact us.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Supabase — cloud storage and authentication.
  • Google (Sign-In, Play Billing, AdMob) — sign-in, subscriptions, and ads.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.

What the app never does

  • It does not give trading signals or entry/exit recommendations.
  • It does not connect to brokers or execute real orders.
  • It does not guarantee or imply that using the app will help you pass a prop firm evaluation.

Your rights

You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by writing to the contact email below. You can also sign out and stop syncing at any time from General Settings inside the app.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will post any changes on this same page with a new update date.
