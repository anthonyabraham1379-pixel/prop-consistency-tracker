Delete account and data

Prop Consistency Tracker · com.anthonyaal.propconsistencytracker

On this page you can request the deletion of your Prop Consistency Tracker account and all data associated with it. You don't need to have the app installed to do so.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to a.isis.a0412@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Write to us from the Google address you signed in with in the app, so we can verify the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm the deletion by email within 30 days.

What data gets deleted

  • Your account (the email address linked to your Google sign-in).
  • All your configured accounts/challenges and their day/trade history synced to the cloud.
  • Any support data associated with your account.

Deletion is permanent and irreversible.

What data may be retained

  • Subscription records: purchase history is managed by Google Play and RevenueCat and may be retained as long as legal and accounting obligations require.
  • Local device data: data saved only on your phone (if you used the app without signing in) isn't on our servers; you can delete it by uninstalling the app or from Android Settings → Apps → Prop Consistency Tracker → Clear data.

Just want to stop syncing?

If you don't want to delete your account, you can sign out at any time from General Settings inside the app. Your data will stop syncing and will remain available locally on your device.

Contact

For any questions about this process or your data, write to us at a.isis.a0412@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
