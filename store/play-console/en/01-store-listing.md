## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Prop Consistency Tracker is a tracking journal for traders running
evaluations and funded accounts with prop firms (FTMO, Apex, TopStep,
Tradeify, and any other). Log your daily result in seconds and see in
real time how you're doing against your challenge rules.

WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU

📊 Real-time consistency rule
Calculates what percentage of your total profit your best day represents,
and warns you when a day is getting close to breaking your firm's
consistency limit (40%, 30%, or whatever you configure).

📉 Drawdown tracking
Supports both static and trailing drawdown. You always know how much
cushion you have left.

🎯 Progress toward your goal
Profit target, minimum profitable days, and a suggested daily goal, all
visible on a clear dashboard.

🗓️ Calendar and history
Review your days in a calendar view (week, month, year), edit or delete
entries, and analyze your performance with stats.

🏦 Multi-account
Track several accounts or challenges at once, each with its own rules.
Every prop firm is different, so every parameter here is configurable.

☁️ Optional sync
Sign in with Google to back up your data and have it on every device. No
sign-in needed either — your data is saved on your phone.

FREE AND PREMIUM

The free version includes 1 active account and 30-day history, with ads.
Premium ($3.99 USD/month) unlocks unlimited accounts, unlimited history,
CSV export, configurable alerts, and removes ads.

IMPORTANT — WHAT THIS APP IS NOT

Prop Consistency Tracker is only a manual logging and tracking tool. It is
not financial or investment advice. It does not connect to brokers or
trading platforms, does not execute trades, does not give signals or
buy/sell recommendations, and does not guarantee or improve the outcome of
any evaluation. The data you see in the app is whatever you enter
yourself. Trading financial instruments carries risk of loss.

Prop Consistency Tracker is not affiliated with any prop firm. Firm names
are mentioned only as examples of parameter compatibility.

Questions or suggestions? Reach us — contact email is on the store
listing.
```
